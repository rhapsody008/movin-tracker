# T5.1a: check-in store, texts and keyboard (pure code)

## Goal
The parts of Go's morning check-in that have no I/O beyond the object store:
- the `checkin.json` claim store, whose bytes are shared with Go;
- the due window;
- the summary and ask texts;
- the `ci:` keyboard and callback parsing.

T5.1b (the adapter's schedule task) and T5.1c (the tick, `/checkin`, callbacks, admin view) build on it.

## Golden
The generator runs Go's real functions. Copy unexported ones verbatim with `file:line` cites, as `t43`/`t44` did. It prints:
- **The summary and asks** for six agendas:
  - empty today;
  - 3 asks;
  - 10 asks (the cap);
  - 7 overdue;
  - one labelled `Yesterday`;
  - one with a room and a time.
- **The keyboard bodies.**
- **The record bytes** for `sending`, `sent` and `quiet`, each with and without `since`/`chat`.
- **The `checkinDue` table:** before the time, at the minute, after it, at and after 20:00, and the day already finished.
- **`parseCheckinCallback`** inputs and results, including malformed ones.

The TS output must be byte-identical (`cmp`), and the generator must be byte-stable on a second run.

## Mutations (at least 8, on container copies)
1. `at` with milliseconds.
2. `since` always written.
3. `taken` true for an unparsable `at`.
4. A takeover that drops `since`.
5. `release` stamping a new `at`.
6. Due as `>` at the minute.
7. The count taken from the capped list.
8. The button date taken from today.
